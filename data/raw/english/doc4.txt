Lucy, also known as Dinkenesh in Amharic, is a famous fossilized hominid discovered in the Hadar region of Ethiopia's Awash Valley in 1974. Belonging to the species Australopithecus afarensis, Lucy lived approximately 3.2 million years ago. She is one of the most complete hominid skeletons ever found, providing crucial evidence for human evolution and early bipedalism, earning Ethiopia the title 'Cradle of Humankind'.
