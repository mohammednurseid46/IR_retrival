Ethiopian runners are globally renowned for their dominance in long-distance running and athletics. Legendary athletes like Abebe Bikila, who won the 1960 Rome Olympic marathon barefoot, paved the way. Haile Gebrselassie, Kenenisa Bekele, and Tirunesh Dibaba have set multiple world records and won numerous Olympic gold medals. The high-altitude training in regions like Bekoji contributes to their exceptional endurance.
