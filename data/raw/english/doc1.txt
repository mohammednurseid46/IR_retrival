Coffee is Ethiopia's gift to the world. Originating in the Kaffa region, coffee arabica grows wild in the highlands. Ethiopian coffee ceremony is an important cultural ritual involving roasting green coffee beans, brewing them in a traditional clay pot called a jebena, and serving it in small cups. Major coffee varieties include Yirgacheffe, Harar, and Sidamo, known for their unique floral and fruity flavors. Coffee is a major export for the Ethiopian economy.
