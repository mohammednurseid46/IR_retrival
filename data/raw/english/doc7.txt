Axum is an ancient city in northern Ethiopia, once the center of the powerful Aksumite Kingdom. It is famous for its towering granite obelisks, or stelae, dating back to the 3rd and 4th centuries AD. The largest standing obelisk is the Obelisk of Axum, which was returned from Italy in 2005. Axum is also believed by many to house the Ark of the Covenant in the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion.
