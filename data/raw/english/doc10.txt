Injera is the staple sourdough flatbread of Ethiopian cuisine, made from teff flour, a tiny, nutrient-rich grain. It has a spongy texture and a slightly sour taste. Injera is served on a large platter with various stews called wat, including Doro Wat (spicy chicken stew) and vegetarian options. Diners eat with their hands, tearing off pieces of injera to scoop up the flavorful stews.
