Bahir Dar University (BDU) is a public university located in Bahir Dar, the capital city of the Amhara Region in Ethiopia. The university offers multiple undergraduate and graduate programs across faculties such as Water Resources, Engineering, Business, Social Sciences, and Education. Established to support regional development and research, BDU hosts research centers and promotes community engagement, innovation, and sustainable development initiatives. The campus is known for its proximity to Lake Tana and historic monasteries, and it attracts students from across the country.
