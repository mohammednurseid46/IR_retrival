The Blue Nile Falls, known locally as Tis Abay (meaning 'smoking fire'), is a spectacular waterfall on the Blue Nile River in Ethiopia. Located near the city of Bahir Dar, the falls cascade down a 45-meter high cliff, creating a massive mist and beautiful rainbows. The river flows from Lake Tana and is a major tributary of the Nile River, playing a crucial role in downstream agriculture.
