Lalibela is famous for its eleven monolithic rock-hewn churches, carved out of solid red volcanic rock in the 12th and 13th centuries. Built during the reign of King Lalibela, these churches are active places of pilgrimage for Ethiopian Orthodox Christians. The most famous is Bete Giyorgis (Church of St. George), carved in the shape of a cross. Lalibela is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a symbol of ancient engineering and architecture.
