Addis Ababa is the capital city of Ethiopia and the diplomatic hub of Africa, hosting the African Union headquarters and the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa. Founded in 1886 by Emperor Menelik II and Empress Taytu Betul, the city lies at the foot of Mount Entoto. It is a vibrant metropolis combining modern skyscrapers, historical sites, and Mercato, one of the largest open-air markets in Africa.
